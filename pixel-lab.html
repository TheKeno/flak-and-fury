Colour

How it works

Left button draws, right button picks the colour under the pointer. Wheel zooms, middle button (or Space and drag) moves the picture. Saving writes data/pixels/<name>.json; a sprite the game uses is rebuilt at once (reload the game to see it). The size is fixed: the game's hit box and the map editor's piece are tied to it.

The bright magenta, cyan and yellow swatches are paint keys: the game swaps them for a nation's colours on vehicles and planes. Scenery does not need them.
