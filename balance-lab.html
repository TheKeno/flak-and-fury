Train bots

Bots learn how to fly each fighter. The trainer tries each plane's tactics (fighting style, height over the enemy, lowest fight speed, breaking off, firing range, throttle in turns) one step at a time in short 2-against-2 air fights against all six, and keeps a change only when it wins by more than luck. The new tactics are saved by themselves (data/bot-tactics.json); Restore puts the previous ones back. Plane data is never changed. A quick run of the fighters starts by itself afterwards.
