## Fighters and attackers

**P-38J** [W-38]
- Engine: V-1710-89/91 (left/right, handed), same as the H but with a core intercooler in the chin radiator allowing higher boost: CONFIRMED. hp for -89/91 not given (the J prototypes' -F17 are 1,425 hp each): hp UNVERIFIED. Single choice: CONFIRMED.
- Guns: 4x .50 (500 rpg) + 1x 20 mm Hispano (150 rounds): CONFIRMED (E-onward standard).
- Rockets: M10 triple tubes (4.5 in) and 5 in HVAR are in the type's spec list, but the zero-length launchers and "Christmas tree" racks (5 per wing) are P-38L (1944-45). CORRECTED: "10x HVAR" is a late L fit, not a 1943 J option.
- Bombs: inner hardpoints 2x 1,000 lb (or drop tanks), outer 500/250 lb: CONFIRMED in the type's general spec; no J-specific page statement. 2,000 lb total on the L.
- Empty weight: 12,800 lb (5,806 kg) is the L; J not given: UNVERIFIED.

**A-36A Apache** [W-A36]
- Engine V-1710-87, 1,325 hp: CONFIRMED. 6x .50 (4 wing, 2 nose): CONFIRMED. Bombs up to 1,000 lb total on two underwing points: CORRECTED (notes said 2x 500 lb; that is consistent, max total 1,000 lb). Empty weight not given (gross 10,000 lb / 4,535 kg).

**Hurricane IIC / IID** [W-Hur]
- IIC: Merlin XX 1,185 hp at 21,000 ft; 4x 20 mm Hispano Mk II; 2x 250 or 500 lb bombs (later wings a hardpoint added); empty 5,745 lb (2,606 kg): CONFIRMED.
- IID: 2x 40 mm in underwing pods + 1x .303 per wing (not 2x .303); first Rolls-Royce 40 mm (12 rounds), then Vickers S (15 rounds): CORRECTED (the notes' "2x 40 mm + 2x .303" is right in count; the IID carried 2 guns, one per wing, plus the 2 Brownings). Engine Merlin XX assumed same as IIC, not stated for IID: UNVERIFIED. 296 built, Jan 1942-Feb 1943; Tunisia use spring 1943: CONFIRMED. No rockets on IIC/IID (RP-3 is the Mk IV): CONFIRMED.
- Weights: Vickers S about 150 kg [W-VickersS]. IID loaded 8,540 lb (3,870 kg) limit.

**Pe-3 (Pe-3bis)** [W-Pe3] (already in the earlier pass, re-stated)
- Engine M-105RA 1,100 hp (820 kW) each; "PF" name UNVERIFIED. Guns: 1x ShVAK (250 rds) + 2x UBK (500 rds total) nose, UBT dorsal (300), ShKAS tail (250, later removed); late 1944 batch lighter. Bombs max 700 kg (prototype: 250 kg per fuselage rack and 100 kg per nacelle). Empty 5,858 kg.

**Il-2M3** [W-Il2]
- Engine AM-38F 1,280 kW (1,720 hp) take-off, 1,100 kW (1,500 hp) at 750 m: CONFIRMED.
- Guns: 2x VYa-23 (150 rpg) + 2x ShKAS (750 rpg) + UBT 12.7 rear (300): CONFIRMED. ShVAK is the earlier single-seat Il-2 and was phased out in 1943: CORRECTED. NS-37 only on the Il-2-37 pod variant (rare): CONFIRMED.
- Rockets/bombs: 8x RS-82 or 4x RS-132; six 100 kg bombs (wing bays + underwing) or 4 dispensers x 48 PTAB-2.5 = 192: CONFIRMED.
- Empty weight 4,425 kg. Guns: ShVAK 40-44.5 kg depending on mount [W-ShVAK]; ShKAS about 10-11 kg [W-ShKAS] (about 40 kg with 650 rounds).

**Ki-45 KAI** [W-45]
- Engine: 2x Ha-102, 783 kW (1,050 hp) in the specs, 805 kW (1,080 hp) in the text: CONFIRMED.
- KAI-a: 2x 12.7 Ho-103 nose, 20 mm Ho-3 belly, 7.92 mm rear. KAI-b: belly 37 mm Type 94 instead of the Ho-3. KAI-c: 37 mm Ho-203 in the nose, 7.92 mm rear: CONFIRMED. 2x 250 kg underwing bombs (not tied to a variant): CONFIRMED. Empty 4,000 kg (KAI-c only). "20 mm Ho-5" belongs to the KAI-d night fighter: CORRECTED.
- Weights: Ho-103 23 kg [W-Ho103].

**D3A2 Val** [W-D3A]
- Engine Kinsei 54, about 1,300 hp take-off (D3A1: Kinsei 43 about 1,000 hp or Kinsei 44 about 1,070 hp): CONFIRMED.
- Guns 2x Type 97 7.7 mm fixed + 1x Type 92 7.7 mm rear flexible; bombs 1x 250 kg centre + 2x 60 kg wing: CONFIRMED (typical load of the type). Empty 2,570 kg (D3A2).

**Zero** [W-A6M]
- A6M2: Sakae 12, 700 kW (940 hp); 2x Type 97 (500 rpg) + 2x Type 99-1 Mk.3 (60 rpg); 2x 60 kg, or one 250 kg (kamikaze fit): CONFIRMED. Empty 1,680 kg.
- A6M3: Model 32/22 Sakae 21, 831 kW (1,130 hp), two-stage supercharger; Model 32 got the Type 99 Mk 2 (100 rpg): CONFIRMED.
- A6M5 (52): Sakae 21; Type 99-2 Mk.3 drum, 100 rpg. A6M5a (52 Ko): belt-fed Mk.4, 125 rpg. A6M5b (Otsu): right 7.7 replaced by a 13.2 mm Type 3 (240 rounds) [13.2 mm, not 12.7: CORRECTED earlier doubt]. A6M5c (Hei): extra 13.2 mm in each wing, left cowl 7.7 removed. Sakae 31 only on some A6M5c per some sources, A6M6/A6M7: partly UNVERIFIED. Model 52 bombs: 250 kg centreline only in bomber conversions: CORRECTED (not a standard 1943 fit). Note 5a/5b/5c are 1944.
- Weights: Type 99 Mk 1 23 kg; Mk 2 34-38 kg (sources conflict) [W-T99].

## Bombers and heavies

**Blenheim Mk IV / IF** [W-Blen]
- Engine: 2x Mercury XV, 920 hp each in the spec box (905 hp in the variant text): CONFIRMED.
- Gun pack: the IF (converted Mk I, about 200) and IVF (about 60 converted Mk IV) carried a belly pack of 4x .303 Browning: CONFIRMED. Mk IV defence: 1 fixed .303 in the port wing, 2x .303 in a dorsal turret, 1-2x .303 rear-firing under the nose: CONFIRMED. Bombs: 1,000 lb internal bay (4x 250 lb or 2x 500 lb), up to 8x 40 lb external; spec box total 1,200 lb: CONFIRMED.
- Finnish service: first export customer; 97 flown (75 Mk I, 22 Mk IV); 55 built locally to September 1944; Series VI (10 Finnish-built Mk IV) up to 1,800 lb in the bay + 379 lb on wing/fuselage racks: CONFIRMED. A Finnish Blenheim gun pack (IF) is UNVERIFIED: the page ties it to RAF conversions, not Finnish aircraft.
- Empty 9,790 lb (4,441 kg).

**Ju 88 A-4** [W-88]
- Engine: Jumo 211J-1/J-2, 1,050 kW (1,410 hp): CONFIRMED.
- Guns: 4x MG 81 (1,000 rpg): one front windscreen flexible, one lower nose, two aft in the canopy, plus an MG 81Z twin in the Bola; some later aircraft carried MG 131: CONFIRMED (MG 131 on the A-4 itself UNVERIFIED; the page mentions it for the C-6).
- Bombs: internal up to 1,400 kg (two bays, 900 kg + 500 kg); external up to 3,000 kg on four racks; max bomb load 3,000 kg, standard 1,500-2,000 kg; max load usually needed rocket-assisted take-off: CORRECTED (notes' "4x SC 500 external or many SC 50 internal" is plausible but only the totals are sourced; SC designations UNVERIFIED).
- Empty 9,860 kg.

**B-17F** [W-17]
- Engine: 4x Wright R-1820 Cyclone: the -97 at 1,200 hp is the G page's figure (the F used the -97 per general knowledge, not on the page): UNVERIFIED for the F specifically. The F standardised the Sperry ball turret; final Douglas-built F blocks adopted the chin turret idea of the YB-40: CONFIRMED.
- Guns: the page gives the G only: 13x .50 M2 in 9 positions (7 to 13 from earlier models to G). F layout: UNVERIFIED (a typical F count of 9-11 is not on the page).
- Bombs (G page): short range 8,000 lb internal; long range (about 800 mi) 4,500 lb; absolute max 17,600 lb internal+external. F: UNVERIFIED. Empty weight G 16,391 kg.
- Weights: AN/M2 .50 about 28 kg; rate 750-850 rpm [W-M2].

**Lancaster B.I / III / II** [W-Lanc]
- B.I: 4x Merlin XX, 1,280 hp each; Merlin 24 on Grand Slam aircraft; B.III: Packard-built Merlin 28 or 38; B.II: Bristol Hercules VI or XVI radials (300 built by Armstrong Whitworth): CONFIRMED (Hercules hp not given).
- Turrets (B.I): nose FN-5A 2x .303, mid-upper FN-50 (later FN-150) 2x .303, ventral FN-64 2x .303 (often removed), tail FN-20 (later FN-120) 4x .303: CONFIRMED (8 guns: 2+2+4; 10 with ventral).
- Bomb bay: unobstructed, about 33 ft (10 m); standard max 14,000 lb (6,400 kg), specialist 22,000 lb (10,000 kg); Cookie 4,000 lb (1,800 kg); about 30% of B.I had bulged doors for 8,000 and later 12,000 lb Cookies; usual load 1 Cookie + 12 SBC (2,832 incendiaries): CONFIRMED.
- Empty weight 16,738 kg (B.I).

**He 177 A-3** [W-177]
- Engines: first A-3s DB 606 A/B (about 2,700 PS), from the 16th aircraft DB 610 A/B (two coupled DB 605); DB 610 hp not given in the guns part; the A-5/R2 table lists 2,218 kW (2,975 hp) each for the DB 610: CONFIRMED (hp from the A-5 page).
- Guns: MG 81 flexible nose; manned dorsal turret with an MG 131; FDL 131Z remote forward dorsal turret 2x MG 131; tail one MG 131 (often MG 151/20); Bola gondola with forward MG 151 (MG FF in early) and a rear ventral gun: CONFIRMED. Exact A-3 totals UNVERIFIED.
- Bombs: Steinbock crews typically 2x 1,800 kg + 2x 1,000 kg; A-5/R2 spec: internal max 7,000 kg plus up to 2,500 kg underwing; A-3/R3 carried Hs 293 glide bombs: CONFIRMED. A-3 internal max itself UNVERIFIED. Empty 16,800 kg is the A-5/R2.

**Pe-8** [W-Pe8]
- Engines: standard 4x AM-35A, 999 kW (1,340 hp) each; earlier AM-34FRN/FRNV; Charomskiy M-40 diesels on at least nine in 1941; ACh-30B diesels only on the 1944 Pe-8ON VIP transports; from late 1942 ASh-82 (1,850 hp): CORRECTED (the ACh-30B was not a 1943 bomber option).
- Guns: dorsal and tail turrets 2x 20 mm ShVAK each, nacelle 2x 12.7 UBT, nose turret 2x 7.62 ShKAS; late ASh-82 aircraft lost nacelle and tail guns, nose turret replaced by a hand-aimed ShKAS: CONFIRMED.
- Bombs: bay up to 4,000 kg; one FAB-500 rack under each wing; one FAB-5000 in a modified bay (second prototype); max 5,000 kg: CONFIRMED. Empty 18,571 kg.

**G4M1** [W-G4M]
- Engine: 2x Kasei 11, 1,140 kW (1,530 hp) take-off: CONFIRMED.
- Guns: tail 1x 20 mm Type 99; 4x 7.7 mm Type 92 (nose 1, top turret 1, 2 waist): CONFIRMED.
- Bombs: one 858 kg Type 91 Kai-3 torpedo, or one 800 kg bomb, or four 250 kg; (Ohka 1,200 kg is a later G4M2e use): CONFIRMED. Bay arrangement not described: UNVERIFIED. Empty 6,741 kg.
