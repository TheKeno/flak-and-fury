# Hangar options: verification pass (2026-11-06)

Checks `notes/hangar-options.md` against web sources. Status per item: CONFIRMED / CORRECTED / UNVERIFIED.
Source quality: Wikipedia and its cited material are the base; where only forums, modelling or game wikis were found
this is said. No primary manuals were reached. Source keys at the end.

## In the game now

**Bf 109 G-6** [W-109, N-109, E-109]
- Engine: DB 605A standard, 1,475 PS take-off, limited to 1,310 PS until 8 June 1943: CONFIRMED [W-109]. The 605AS and 605AM were not checked (UNVERIFIED; AM/MW 50 belongs to 1944, not 1943).
- Guns: /U4 MK 108 30 mm through the hub: CONFIRMED [W-109]. R6 = two MG 151/20 underwing gondolas, 135 rpg, +212 kg: CONFIRMED [W-109, E-109]. Notes' "MK 108" gondolas are R4 (two MK 108 gunpods): CONFIRMED [W-109]. Also real: Wfr.Gr. 21 rocket tubes, one under each wing, 40.8 kg warhead, used on the G-5, G-6, G-14 [W-109] (the notes list rockets for the 110 only).
- Bombs: R1 = ETC 501/IX b, one SC 250 or SD 250 only. The 500 kg claim is CORRECTED: no 500 kg on the G-6 (ground clearance; the 500 kg figure is the G-2/R1 prototype with jettisonable auxiliary undercarriage, and the K-4) [W-109, N-109, forum F-109]. R2 = ETC 50/VIII d, 4x SC 50: CONFIRMED [W-109]. R3 = 300 l drop tank rack, not a bomb: CONFIRMED.

**Bf 109 F-4**
- Engine: DB 601E, 1,350 PS (restricted to 1,200 PS at first; cleared Feb 1942): CONFIRMED [W-109].
- Guns: 2x MG 17 + MG 151/20 engine gun: CONFIRMED. Gondolas are /R1: two MG 151/20, 135 rpg, about 240 built early 1942: CORRECTED ("rare" is right but the kit is R1, and R6 is the G-6 name) [W-109].
- Bombs: Wikipedia names no F-4 bomb variant; the rack data given is for the E-4/B (ETC 500: one 250 kg or four 50 kg). F-4/B 250 kg or 4x SC 50 is plausible but UNVERIFIED for the F-4 itself.

**Spitfire Mk IX**
- Engines: F = Merlin 61/63, LF = Merlin 66, HF = Merlin 70; F production halted late 1943 for LF: CONFIRMED (secondary sources, H-spit; I did not fetch Wikipedia successfully). "Most built" for the LF is UNVERIFIED.
- Wings: C wing takes 8x .303, 2 cannon + 4x .303, or 4 cannon; E wing = 2 Hispano + 2x .50: CONFIRMED [H-spit]. Notes' wing types A/B are the older names (A, B, C wing numbering): C-wing covers all three loads in 1943 practice. The E wing came later (about 1944): date UNVERIFIED.
- Bombs: 1x 500 lb centreline or 2x 250 lb wing racks: CONFIRMED [H-spit]. Combined centre + wing load: UNVERIFIED.

**P-47D**
- Engines: R-2800-59, 2,000 hp, 2,300 hp with water injection: CONFIRMED [W-47]. -21 for early D: UNVERIFIED. Water injection starts around D-4/D-5, not D-22: CORRECTED (D-11 only changed the control) [S-47].
- Guns: 8x .50 standard; a 6-gun production P-47D is not supported by any source found: CORRECTED (drop the "6x .50 occasionally"). Rockets: M10 triple tubes (4.5 in) exist; 5 in HVAR from 1944: CONFIRMED [W-47].
- Bombs: early D 500 lb belly; mid-production up to 1,000 lb; max ordnance 2,500 lb [W-47]. Wing racks (B-10, tank or 1,000 lb) came with the D-15 block (spring 1944, forum source): CORRECTED, so "late 1943/44" is early; fine for a 1944-only wing rack.

**La-5FN**
- Engine: M-82FN, 1,460 kW (1,960 hp), fuel injected: CONFIRMED [W-La5].
- Guns: 2x ShVAK 20 mm, 170 rpg; B-20 only in very late production (reported): CONFIRMED, treat B-20 as UNVERIFIED for 1943 [W-La5].
- Bombs: two bombs up to 100 kg each: CORRECTED ("2x 50, maybe 2x 100" becomes 2x up to 100 kg; game FAB-100 fits) [W-La5]. RS-82 on La-5FN: UNVERIFIED (not in the article).

**Ki-43-II**
- Engine: Ha-115 (Ha-25 with two-stage supercharger, longer cowl), about 1,150 hp: CONFIRMED [W-43].
- Guns: 2x Ho-103 12.7 mm on all IIa/IIb/II Kai: CONFIRMED [W-43]. Ho-5 20 mm only on the two prototype IIIb: not an option.
- Bombs: 2x 30 kg or 2x 250 kg (IIb spec in Wikipedia); other sources: 2x 50, 2x 100 or 2x 250 kg (Army Type 94/92) [W-43, game wiki]. CORRECTED: 250 kg is well supported, 30 kg weakly; 50/100 kg options also appear. Wikipedia's "up to 500 kg" is the total for the two racks.

**Brewster B-239 (Finland)**
- Engine: Wright R-1820-G5, 950 hp (some sources 1,000 hp): CONFIRMED [W-F2A, F-bw]. The B-339 had the G-105.
- Guns: CORRECTED. Delivered as 1x .30 + 1x .50 nose, 2x .50 wings; the Finns fitted four .50 (12.7 mm); by 1943 all but one carried four .50. The game's 4x .50 is right; no .303 mix in Finnish service [W-F2A, F-bw].
- Bombs: none found for the Finnish 239 (only the US F2A-3 had 2x 100 lb, which is not the 239): UNVERIFIED, keep "none".

**Bf 110 G-2**
- Engine: DB 605B, 1,475 PS emergency: CONFIRMED [W-110].
- Guns: baseline 4x MG 17 + 2x MG 151/20 + MG 81Z (the MG 81Z rear is confirmed; the nose set is the standard G). R1 = BK 3,7 (37 mm) in a conformal pod under the fuselage: CONFIRMED [W-110]. R4 = BK 3,7 plus 2x MK 108 in the nose: CONFIRMED [W-110]. MK 108 replacing the MG 17s (R3 in one source): CONFIRMED in principle, kit number varies by source. R-number lists conflict between sources: UNVERIFIED which number is which beyond R1/R4.
- Rockets: "often equipped with rockets" [W-110]; WGr.21 tubes are standard G-series practice, but no G-2 kit number found: UNVERIFIED.
- Bombs: Wikipedia gives bomb data only for the C-1/B (2x 250/500/1000 kg on ETC 500) and E-0 (4x SC 50 under the wing). A G-2 fuselage rack with 2x SC 250 or 2x SC 500 is UNVERIFIED for the G-2 (an ETC 500/IX b is rated 2x SC 500 or 2x SC 250 per a forum list).

**Mosquito FB.VI**
- Engines: Merlin 21 (1,460 hp) or 25 (1,635 hp) per Wikipedia; one Wikipedia infobox line also gives Mk 23: Merlin 21/23/25 is CORRECTED to 21 (early) and 25 (Series 2), 23 UNVERIFIED [W-Mos].
- Guns: 4x Hispano + 4x .303: CONFIRMED [W-Mos].
- Bombs: bay takes two 250 lb or 500 lb, not four 250 lb (the four-bomb bay is the B.IV and Mk VI had it shortened for the cannon): CORRECTED. Wings: one 250 lb or 500 lb under each wing; Series 1 only 250 lb, Series 2 allowed 500 lb [W-Mos]. Total up to 2,000 lb (910 kg) [W-Mos]. The notes' "bay 2x 250 (or 500)" is right; "wings 2x 250 or 500" is right.
- RP-3: 8 rockets (25 lb or 60 lb heads) on the wing racks [W-Mos]. Date: Coastal Command FB.VI conversions reportedly from December 1943; first reported attack elsewhere is 1944 [blog; historyofwar]: date UNVERIFIED, game "1943-44" is acceptable, ahead of that is not supported.

**Ju 87 D-5**
- Engine: Jumo 211J, 1,420 PS: CONFIRMED [W-87].
- Guns: wing MG 17 replaced by MG 151/20, rear MG 81Z; wings 0.6 m longer: CONFIRMED [W-87]. G-1: converted from D-series, two BK 3,7 pods, first at front April 1943, no dive brakes: CONFIRMED [W-87]. Notes' "D-5 with 2x BK 37 pods" is CORRECTED: that is the G-1, a separate type, not a D-5 option.
- Bombs: Revell manual, as read in search: normal 1x SC 1000 under the fuselage, or 1x SC 500 + 4x SC 50/70; overload 1x SC 1800 short range; Wikipedia: overload up to 1,800 kg, typical 500-1,200 kg [W-87, F-87]. CORRECTED: SC 1000 is a normal D load, not an overload; SC 1800 is the overload. "SC 250 + 4x SC 50" UNVERIFIED. Wing rack capacity: sources conflict (50 kg vs up to 300 kg): UNVERIFIED.

## Planned

**Il-2M3 (1943)** [W-Il2, F-Il2]
- Engine AM-38F, 1,280 kW take-off: CONFIRMED.
- Guns: 2x VYa-23 (150 rpg) + 2x ShKAS; ShVAK phased out in 1943: CORRECTED (ShVAK is the earlier standard, 1943 default is VYa-23). NS-37 (Il-2-37) pods: ordered April 1943, used at Kursk, limited numbers, poor accuracy: CONFIRMED as a rare option.
- Bombs/rockets: six 100 kg (wing bays and underwing) or four PTAB dispensers with 192 PTAB-2.5-1.5 bomblets; 8x RS-82 or 4x RS-132; PTAB first mass use at Kursk 1943: CORRECTED ("up to 4x 100 kg internal" is wrong; the internal bays held 4x 100 kg in some sources but Wikipedia says six including external). Max 600 kg early, 400 kg on the two-seater.

**Pe-3** [W-Pe3]
- Engine: Wikipedia says M-105RA 1,100 hp (not M-105PF): CORRECTED, 105PF/RA naming UNVERIFIED.
- Guns: production Pe-3 / 3bis: 1x ShVAK 20 mm nose, 2x UBK 12.7 mm nose, 1x UBT 12.7 dorsal, 1x ShKAS tail (later removed); late 1944 batch lighter. Notes' "1x UB 12.7 + ShKAS" is CORRECTED.

**Ki-45 KAI** [W-45]
- Engine: Ha-102, 783-805 kW each: CONFIRMED.
- Guns: KAI-a: nose 2x 12.7 Ho-103, belly 20 mm Ho-3, rear 7.92 mm; KAI-b: belly 37 mm Type 94 instead of the Ho-3; KAI-c: nose 37 mm Ho-203; KAI-d: night fighter with Ho-203 + 2x Ho-5 upward. CORRECTED: the 37 mm is a nose gun only on KAI-c; 20 mm Ho-5 is the Schraege Musik. Bombs: 2x 250 kg underwing: CONFIRMED.

**Zero** [W-A6M, F-A6M]
- A6M2: Sakae 12, 950 hp; 2x Type 97 7.7 mm + 2x Type 99-1 20 mm, 60 rpg; 2x 60 kg (or 30 kg): CONFIRMED.
- A6M3: Model 22 uses the Sakae 21; some Model 22 had the long-barrel Type 99-2 cannon (Model 22 Ko or later: sources differ). Sakae 21 as standard engine for A6M3/A6M5: CONFIRMED. Model 32 (Sakae 21, clipped wings) exists. A6M5: Sakae 21 (Sakae 31 only on late A6M5c/A6M6): the Sakae 31 point UNVERIFIED.
- A6M5b: 1x 13.2 mm Type 3 replaces one 7.7: sources disagree (12.7 vs 13.2); A6M5c adds 13.2 mm guns in the wings: UNVERIFIED.
- Bombs: 2x 60 kg or 1x 250 kg: CONFIRMED.

**Not checked in this pass:** P-38J, A-36A, Hurricane IID, D3A2, Blenheim IV, Ju 88 A-4, heavies. All still UNVERIFIED.
